About SmartUp

Mohamed Rayees Pulikkannattu Anaz is the Managing Director & Chairman of Smart Up Learning Ventures Pvt Ltd, who once borrowed just ₹3,000 from his mother to start a dream — and built a tuition centre that all of Kerala knows today. From Kochi, Kerala, Smartup has been silently shaping the futures of thousands of children for 12 years, with one core belief: education is everywhere, but without a strong base, everything stops halfway. Today, 50,000+ students have learned and grown through Smartup — and many of them now stand in great positions in life. Behind every one of those success stories, one name remains: Smartup. This vision is driven by four founders — Mohamed Rayees Pulikkannattu Anaz (Managing Director & Chairman), Dhanesh Narayanan Achari (Director), Mohamed Shihas Kolothpurakal Yahiya (Director), and Shanu Kattuvallappil Yahiya (Director) — a team united by one mission: to give every child the strong foundation they deserve.

Report Conclusion
  Campaign Summary

Campaign Delivered Outstanding Awareness Results

The Instagram awareness campaign for SmartUp — led by Mohamed Rayees Pulikkannattu Anaz, Managing Director & Chairman of Smart Up Learning Ventures Pvt Ltd — exceeded expectations, successfully achieving its primary objectives of expanding brand visibility and driving strong engagement across a highly targeted Malayalam-speaking audience. With a total reach of 1.3M unique accounts and an average engagement rate of 8.1% — significantly above the industry benchmark of 3–5% — the campaign is a clear testament to the power of authentic, community-driven content.

The 25 partnered pages played a pivotal role in amplifying SmartUp's story and its 12-year legacy of transforming education across Kerala, collectively generating over 646,000 impressions and 17.7k likes. The campaign also received 140+ comments and accumulated 215k story views, reflecting genuine audience interest and conversations around SmartUp's mission to give every child a strong educational foundation.

These results demonstrate that SmartUp's story — a brand born from just ₹3,000 that has now shaped the futures of 50,000+ students across Kerala — resonated deeply with the community. We strongly recommend building on this momentum with a conversion-focused follow-up campaign to translate this broad awareness into enrollments and measurable growth for SmartUp.
